# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads ensure the quality and testability of all deliverables. They design test strategies, facilitate test planning and execution, and serve as the quality gatekeeper for releases.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain test strategies and test plans
- Facilitate test planning and execution across sprints
- Clarify and validate acceptance criteria with Product Managers and Developers
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Collaborate with Developers and Product Managers on validating releases
- Report on quality metrics and test coverage
- Identify quality risks early and recommend mitigation

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable deliverables
- Minimize production defects and regressions
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- Test plan reviews and QA status updates
- Bug triage meetings
- Release readiness discussions

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams. They translate business requirements into actionable user stories, specifications, and process improvements.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and analyze business requirements from stakeholders
- Translate business needs into detailed user stories or technical specs
- Facilitate requirement clarification throughout delivery cycles
- Document business processes and workflows
- Support data analysis and reporting needs
- Validate solutions against business requirements
- Assist with user acceptance testing

### Goals
- Ensure technical solutions align with business objectives
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements
- Improve business process efficiency
- Enable data-driven decision making

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder requirement sessions
- User story refinement meetings
- Process mapping workshops
- Business review presentations

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers define and deliver exceptional user experiences. They create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes that align with user needs and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather feedback
- Define user experience goals and design principles
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Design user interfaces that are intuitive and accessible
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feature requirements
- Work with Developers to ensure design implementation fidelity
- Conduct usability testing and iterate based on feedback
- Maintain design systems and component libraries

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and improve satisfaction
- Ensure design consistency across products
- Validate designs through user testing

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions
- User research findings presentations
- Collaboration with Product Managers on roadmap
- Developer handoff meetings
- Usability testing debriefs

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers automate infrastructure, deployment, and operational processes. They ensure systems are reliable, scalable, and observable while enabling fast, safe delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Automate infrastructure provisioning and configuration
- Ensure system reliability, scalability, and performance
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting solutions
- Manage deployment processes and release automation
- Support incident response and troubleshooting
- Work closely with Developers during release cycles
- Optimize development and deployment workflows

### Goals
- Enable fast, automated, and reliable deployments
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce manual operational toil
- Improve observability and incident response

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Infrastructure and architecture reviews
- Developer tooling feedback sessions

---

## Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic direction and organizational support for projects. They ensure alignment with business strategy, approve resources, and remove high-level blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Provide high-level strategic direction and vision
- Approve project budgets and resource allocations
- Remove organizational and cross-functional blockers
- Ensure alignment with business strategy and priorities
- Make go/no-go decisions at key project gates
- Champion the project across the organization
- Review milestone progress and success metrics
- Support change management and adoption

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver strategic business value
- Provide organizational support and resources
- Maintain alignment with company objectives
- Enable successful project outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based project reviews
- Strategic planning sessions
- Executive stakeholder briefings
- Escalation resolution meetings

## Interaction Matrix

This section describes how the personas interact with each other to facilitate successful project delivery.

### QA Lead interactions:
- ↔️ **Developers**: Test planning, bug triage, quality gates
- ↔️ **Product Managers**: Acceptance criteria validation, quality requirements
- ↔️ **Project Managers**: Test progress reporting, release readiness
- ↔️ **DevOps Engineers**: Test automation infrastructure, deployment validation

### Business Analyst interactions:
- ↔️ **Stakeholders**: Requirements gathering, validation
- ↔️ **Product Managers**: Backlog refinement, prioritization input
- ↔️ **Developers**: User story clarification, technical feasibility
- ↔️ **Project Managers**: Scope definition, timeline estimation
- ↔️ **QA Lead**: Acceptance criteria definition

### UX/UI Designer interactions:
- ↔️ **Product Managers**: Feature definition, user research insights
- ↔️ **Developers**: Design implementation, feasibility discussions
- ↔️ **Business Analysts**: User workflow requirements
- ↔️ **QA Lead**: Usability testing, accessibility validation

### DevOps Engineer interactions:
- ↔️ **Developers**: CI/CD pipeline, deployment support, infrastructure needs
- ↔️ **QA Lead**: Test environment management, automation infrastructure
- ↔️ **Project Managers**: Release planning, deployment timelines
- ↔️ **Executive Sponsor**: Infrastructure investment decisions

### Executive Sponsor interactions:
- ↔️ **Project Managers**: Status updates, escalation resolution
- ↔️ **Product Managers**: Strategic alignment, roadmap priorities
- ↔️ **Stakeholders**: Cross-functional coordination, change management
